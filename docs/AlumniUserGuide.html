1. Getting Started

Open https://reconverge.onrender.com on any device — laptop, tablet, or phone. The site is mobile-friendly, so you can do everything from WhatsApp on the go.

Tip: The site is hosted on a free plan and may take ~30 seconds to wake up the first time you open it after a long gap. Be patient — it's normal.

2. Registering

  1. Click Register / RSVP on the landing page.
  2. Fill in your full name, email (this becomes your login), and a password.
  3. Add your branch, hostel, roll number, current city & company — this helps batchmates find you in the directory.
  4. Enter travel & stay preferences: how you're arriving, dates, room sharing, T-shirt size, dietary preference, and family members coming along.
  5. Click Submit. You'll land on a success page and be logged in automatically.
Important: Use a real email. Duplicate emails are rejected. You can't change the email later — it's your account identity — but every other field is editable from the profile page.

3. Logging In

Already registered? Click Login (top-right), enter your email + password, and you'll land on your profile dashboard. Forgot your password? Ping the admin team on the WhatsApp group — manual resets until self-service is live.

4. Your Profile

Your profile is the single source of truth the organising team uses for T-shirts, meals, rooming, and the cab manifest. Keep it up to date.

Edit profile

From the avatar menu (top-right) → Edit Profile. You can update:

  • Avatar — paste any image URL, or hit Randomise to generate a fun cartoon avatar. Reset to default brings back the auto-generated one based on your name.
  • Personal — name, phone, city, state/country, company, designation.
  • Academic — branch, hostel, roll number.
  • Travel & Stay — mode of travel, arrival/departure date & time, room preference, preferred roommate.
  • Preferences — T-shirt size, dietary preference, adults / children count, special requests, notes.
  • Payment & ID — payment UID after you transfer fees, and last 4 digits of a govt. ID (for campus entry).

Click Save Changes at the bottom. You'll see a green toast confirming the update.

5. Event Schedule

The Itinerary page shows the day-by-day programme — welcome dinner, campus tour, batch photo, gala night, farewell brunch. Each event has a time, venue, and short blurb.

Under My Itinerary, you can mark events you'll attend so the team gets headcounts for catering and seating.

6. Travel Planner

The Travel Planner is a shared board where alumni post their transport plans and find others to share a cab or pick-up with.

  1. Open Travel Planner from the nav.
  2. Click Add travel item — choose arrival or departure, fill in date, time, airport/station, and flight/train number.
  3. Set Visibility:
    • Public — everyone sees it.
    • Batch only — only logged-in alumni.
    • Specific people — pick from the searchable list; only those chosen batchmates see it.
  4. Save. Others can comment on your item to coordinate — "I land 20 minutes before you, let's share a cab."
Tip: Post your plans early. The more people on the board, the easier it is to cluster arrivals and split costs.

7. Stay & Rooming

The Stay page lists on-campus hostel allocation, nearby hotels, and rates. Your room preference from the profile feeds into the rooming chart the organising team prepares.

If you have a specific roommate request, add their name in the Preferred Roommate field of your profile — the team tries to honour pairs where both sides list each other.

8. Alumni Directory

Browse everyone who's registered — filter by branch or hostel, search by name. Click a card to see their current city, company, and (if shared) phone/email. Great for pre-reunion reconnects.

9. Nostalgia — Photos & Videos

The Nostalgia section has two galleries:

  • Photo Gallery — throwback pics from hostel life, fests, trips, farewells. Upload your own from the gallery page.
  • Video Gallery — old clips, montages, and testimonial videos.

Please upload! Every photo someone shares triggers five more memories from the group.

10. Merchandise Store

The Store has reunion hoodies, T-shirts, mugs, and a class-of-2001 coffee-table book. Add to cart → review → note the total. Payment is via the UID you paste into your profile (unified with event fees).

11. Groups & Townhalls

Groups are interest/branch/hostel sub-circles — join one, post updates, plan meet-ups within the larger reunion. Townhalls are scheduled virtual sessions before the event — Q&A with the organising committee, logistics run-throughs, sponsor announcements.

12. Give Back

The Give Back page lists causes the batch is collectively supporting — campus infra, scholarships, a memorial fund. Pledge any amount; every rupee is acknowledged on the honour roll.

13. FAQ & Help

The FAQ page covers the top 20 questions — payment, accommodation, family members, cancellations, campus access. If your question isn't there, ping the organising team on the WhatsApp group or use the contact form on the landing page.
